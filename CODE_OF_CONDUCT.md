# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive in all project interactions.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith; resolve disagreements with evidence and discussion.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, and publishing
others' private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the repository maintainers via GitHub.
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.
